People

A collaborative group of ecologists, geneticists, modelers, and field biologists working across three continents.

Principal Investigator
Taal Levi
Professor · Department of Fisheries, Wildlife & Conservation Sciences

Taal leads the Levi Lab at Oregon State University. The lab applies molecular and quantitative methods to address basic and applied problems in wildlife ecology, biodiversity science, and conservation. Research spans the Neotropics, Alaska, and the Pacific Northwest — from the community ecology of Lyme disease to jaguar ecology in the Amazon to salmon-supported bear populations in Southeast Alaska.

The lab maintains a high-throughput molecular facility for DNA metabarcoding, genotyping by amplicon sequencing, and environmental DNA assays, and partners with the Pacific Northwest Bioacoustics Laboratory for passive acoustic monitoring of wildlife.
