About the Lab

Basic & Applied Research
Across Three Continents

We apply molecular and quantitative methods to address fundamental and pressing problems in wildlife ecology, biodiversity science, and conservation—from the Neotropics to Alaska to Oregon.

Our work spans environmental genetics, trophic cascades, salmon ecology, disease ecology, and human-wildlife systems. We develop novel molecular tools, statistical models, and AI-assisted approaches to understand and protect the natural world.
